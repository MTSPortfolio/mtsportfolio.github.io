Background

How I got here

The analyst half

I work as a Senior Data Analyst in the telco industry in Chile, where the job is making large amounts of operational data answer specific business questions. That's where I learned that the hard part is rarely the calculation — it's deciding what's worth calculating, and presenting it so someone can act on it.

The developer half

Analysis that lives in a notebook helps one person once. So I started shipping the models as web applications instead: same maths, but with a URL, a test suite, and a deploy pipeline. Three of them are in production today, and I run the server they sit on.
